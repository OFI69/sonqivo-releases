# SONQIVO – What's new

This lists the changes you notice when using the program. Versions without visible changes are not listed.
This English list starts with version 1.10.0; earlier entries exist in German only.

## 1.13.0 – 2026-11-16
**Changed**
- “Contact” and “Report a bug” now work by email only (kontakt@sonqivo.de).

## 1.12.0 – 2026-11-14
**New**
- Alphabet bar “# A B C … Z” above the song list: one click jumps straight to the initial letter, no scrolling.
- Volume as a slider below the waveform, reachable in every tab (0–150 %, double-click = 100 %).
- The “About SONQIVO” window shows the web address sonqivo.de.

**Changed**
- The contact address is now kontakt@sonqivo.de.
- The volume knob in the “Tempo & Pitch” tab is gone (the slider below the waveform replaces it).
- Minimum window size 1000 × 700; the song list gets a little more room.

## 1.11.1 – 2026-11-13
**Changed**
- Tempo trainer redesigned: two cards, percent and BPM shown large, progress bar and a “Start trainer” button.

## 1.11.0 – 2026-11-12
**New**
- Tempo trainer: “From xx % up to max. xx %” with a “Start trainer” button. After a few passes of the loop the tempo rises step by step. Percent and BPM are shown large, also next to the song title.
- Language German / English under “System” → “Settings” (applies after a restart). On first start SONQIVO follows the Windows language.

## 1.10.1 – 2026-11-11
**New**
- Installer for Windows (SONQIVO-Setup.exe): no administrator rights needed, desktop icon optional. On uninstall you are asked whether your data is kept. The version without installation remains available as a ZIP.

**Changed**
- SONQIVO starts faster. Songs and settings stay unchanged.

## 1.10.0 – 2026-11-10
**New**
- “System” → “Third-party licenses …”: list of the free software components contained in SONQIVO.
